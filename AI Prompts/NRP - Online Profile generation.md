## Analysis Framework

**Please evaluate evidence for the following models, clearly distinguishing between observable behaviors and theoretical inferences:**

### 1. Stages / Levels (formerly Spiral Dynamics Values Systems)
- Analyze communication patterns, stated values, and decision-making approaches
- Identify potential primary and secondary levels with confidence ratings
- Note: Focus on expressed values rather than assumed developmental stages

### 2. Big Five (OCEAN) Personality Traits
- **Openness**: Creative expression, intellectual curiosity, artistic interests
- **Conscientiousness**: Work habits, reliability, goal orientation
- **Extraversion**: Social behavior, energy sources, communication style
- **Agreeableness**: Interpersonal approach, cooperation, empathy indicators
- **Neuroticism**: Stress responses, emotional regulation patterns

### 3. MBTI Cognitive Functions
- Analyze decision-making patterns and information processing
- Look for evidence of dominant and auxiliary functions
- Provide type hypothesis with reasoning, not certainty

### 4. Enneagram Core Type
- Identify potential core motivations and fears
- Look for stress/security patterns if observable
- Note: Distinguish between public persona and core drivers

### 5. Multiple Intelligences (Gardner)
- Identify demonstrated strengths across intelligence types
- Base on observable skills, achievements, and interests

### 6. Both Light and Dark Triad 
- Identify the light triad traits of **Faith in humanity**, **Humanism** and **Kantianism**
- Identify the dark triad traits of **Narcissism**, **Machiavellianism**, and **Psychopathy**

## Quality Standards

**Evidence Requirements:**
- Cite specific examples from public communications, work, or documented behaviors
- Distinguish between direct evidence and reasonable inferences
- Rate confidence levels: High (strong evidence), Medium (some indicators), Low (speculative)


**Output Structure:**
1. **Executive Summary** (2-3 sentences of key insights)
2. **Copyable Report** - As explained with an example later, just the main points.
3. **Detailed Analysis** (organized by framework above)
5. **Limitations & Caveats** (what cannot be determined from available data)
